Peter Thiel's Dialog Network & Leak (2026)

A dossier on the secretive elite network Dialog, co-founded by Peter Thiel and Auren Hoffman, and the June 2026 data leak exposing its member database and controversial agenda items such as 'Build-a-Cult'.

The Dialog Network & June 2026 Leak

Co-founded in 2006 by Peter Thiel and Auren Hoffman, Dialog was established as an exclusive, invitation-only network of business, political, military, and tech leaders, functioning without public oversight for two decades. In June 2026, the network was exposed when Swiss hacktivist maia arson crimew uncovered a directory of participant profiles embedded directly in the HTML source code of Dialog's website.

Simultaneously, an anonymous source provided journalists at WIRED with a registration list for Dialog's upcoming 2026 retreat, scheduled to be held near Dublin, Ireland, in August. The leaked database contained the names, contact details, and affiliations of 222 elite figures, pulling back the veil on a network that has long operated under strict secrecy reminiscent of the Bilderberg Group or the World Economic Forum.

The 'Build-a-Cult' Agenda & Thiel's Philosophy

The leak exposed the retreat's planned agenda, which featured several highly controversial and provocative sessions. Among them was a session titled "Build-a-Cult," slated to be moderated by the founder of the Christian networking platform Pray.com. Other sessions included "Navigating WWIII," "Bring Back Nuclear," "Battlefield Technologies," and "How's Your Sex Life?"

The concept of "building a cult" aligns with Peter Thiel's historical business and leadership philosophy. In his 2014 book Zero to One, Thiel explicitly argued that high-performing startup organizations should resemble "slightly less extreme kinds of cults." He proposed that a "cult-like" culture—defined by extreme dedication, shared loyalty, and a cohesive, intense mission—is a prerequisite for successful, mission-driven institutions to survive and disrupt established paradigms. Critics argue that discussing this framework at an off-the-record summit of military and political elites points to a broader interest in psychological and societal control mechanisms.

Elite Alignment & Global Influence

The leaked participant list revealed the extensive overlap between tech venture capitalists, intelligence agencies, military command, and national political figures. Notable attendees registered for the retreat included U.S. Senator Ted Cruz, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, NATO commander General Alexus Grynkewich, along with various defense contractors, executives, and select media representatives. The discovery of government officials and active military personnel participating in highly secretive, off-the-record meetings hosted by tech billionaires has renewed public debates surrounding the democratic accountability of private elite discussion forums and their influence on national defense and domestic policies.
