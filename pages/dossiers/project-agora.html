Project Agorá

A dossier investigating Project Agorá, a major public-private financial experiment launched in 2024 to integrate commercial bank tokenized deposits with wholesale central bank digital currencies on a unified ledger.

Introduction

Project Agorá (Greek for "marketplace") is a massive public-private partnership launched on April 3, 2024, by the Bank for International Settlements (BIS). The project brings together seven central banks—including the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, the Bank of England, the Bank of Japan, the Banque de France (representing the Eurosystem), the Swiss National Bank, the Bank of Korea, and the Banco de México—alongside a large group of private financial institutions. Project Agorá focuses on tokenizing cross-border wholesale payments, combining commercial bank money with wholesale central bank digital currencies (wCBDCs) within a unified programmable ledger.

The Concept of the Unified Ledger

The core technological innovation of Project Agorá is the "unified ledger." Rather than transferring money across separate databases using messaging systems (such as SWIFT) and settling them later, a unified ledger hosts central bank money, tokenized commercial bank deposits, and smart contracts on the same platform. Transactions are executed and settled simultaneously, eliminating clearing delays and counterparty risk. From a policy perspective, this is framed as a modernization of financial infrastructure. From a critical standpoint, it consolidates transaction recording, compliance monitoring, and sovereign money management into a single, centralized technological stack.

Public-Private Financial Integration

Agorá represents a major pivot in central banking strategy. Rather than bypassing traditional commercial banks with retail CBDCs—which could trigger bank runs and destabilize the financial sector—Project Agorá seeks to co-opt commercial banks by embedding them directly in the tokenization process. In this model, commercial banks issue "tokenized deposits" representing public deposits, which are then cleared internationally against central bank wholesale tokens. This hybrid model preserves the role of existing commercial banks while subjecting their transactions to automated, smart-contract-enforced compliance rules governed by central banks.

Analytical Implications

Project Agorá is critical because it represents the real-world execution of the BIS's blueprint for the future monetary system. By creating an integrated ledger that matches wholesale central bank money with commercial digital deposits, the BIS is building the structural plumbing for a fully programmable global financial network. This plumbing would allow for instant compliance checks, automated tax extractions, and the absolute enforcement of transaction rules. Project Agorá demonstrates that the transition to a cashless society is being organized at the institutional apex of global finance.
