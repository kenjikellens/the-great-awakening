Editorial Standards & Verification

Simple, clear, and direct guidelines for our public-record database.

Our Mission

The Great Awakening Archive is a public-access database of declassified files, official government transcripts, and historical public records. Our goal is to make these important documents easy to find, search, and verify.

We operate strictly under objective, neutral guidelines. We do not write editorial commentary, voice personal opinions, or take political sides. We simply publish verified records and primary sources.

What We Cover

Banking & Money

Articles on central banks, fractional reserve banking, national debt, and digital banking (CBDCs).

Global Governance

Information on international treaties, policy proposals from advisory groups, and international agreements.

Public Policy

Dossiers covering digital identity cards, environmental standards, urban infrastructure, and health protocols.

Historical Records

Proven intelligence programs, government studies, and covert operations that have been officially declassified.

Verification Standards

  • Primary Sources Only:

    Every article must link directly to an official document, original transcript, or public record. We do not host rumors or opinion pieces.

  • Permanent Archiving:

    We save original copies of all indexed documents so they cannot be edited, deleted, or changed later.

  • Fact-Checking:

    All factual claims must be verified against multiple reliable sources before we add them to the database.

  • Open to Correction:

    Anyone can review our database and suggest corrections. We update our articles if you can provide verifiable primary evidence.
