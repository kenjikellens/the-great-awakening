How to Contribute

We welcome public contributions of declassified documents, official government transcripts, and historical records. Use the form to suggest new sources or let us know about any mistakes in our articles.

Submission Guidelines

  • Provide Original Sources: All submissions must include direct links or scanned copies of original public records, government papers, or official transcripts.
  • Keep it Neutral: Avoid personal opinions, gossip, or speculation. We only publish verified, objective facts.
  • Review Process: Our volunteer team reviews every suggestion. Approved records are usually formatted and added to the database within a few days.
