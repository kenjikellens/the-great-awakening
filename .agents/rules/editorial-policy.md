# Editorial Policy: Exposing Institutional Corruption

This platform is dedicated to exposing institutional corruption, elite collusion, corporate-state capture, shadow operations, and globalist agendas. 

## 📝 Editorial Rules

1. **Context Then Corruption:** Articles may briefly explain what a topic is and how it works (e.g., how AI surveillance operates, how a CBDC ledger functions) as an introduction. But the core goal of every article is to expose the corruption, collusion, or agenda behind it — not to simply describe the technology or policy.
2. **Adhere to Critical Angles:** Every dossier in the database `data/dossiers-data.json` contains a `"critical_angle"` metadata key. This key defines the specific narrative focus and exposing angle for that article. Always read and strictly adhere to this specification when creating or editing files.
3. **Wikipedia-Style Inline Linking:** Within the body text of articles, reference other existing dossier topics by linking to them inline (e.g., `<a href="#dossier/eu-ai-act">EU AI Act</a>` or `<a href="#dossier/neuralink">Neuralink</a>`). Do not just list references at the bottom of the article; integrate clickable links directly into relevant keywords throughout the paragraphs to allow seamless cross-navigation.
